Keramos Ceramiche d'Autore

Keramos Ceramiche d'Autore is an advertising campaign inspired by the Italian expression "Belle da morire" ("Beautiful enough to die for"). The concept transforms a familiar idiom into a powerful visual metaphor, positioning ceramic surfaces as the true protagonists of each environment. The result is a memorable campaign that combines irony, elegance and contemporary art direction to reinforce the brand's identity.
